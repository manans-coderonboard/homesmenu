Overview

Perched on the 12th floor of the iconic Skyline Heights tower in the heart of Bandra West, this 3 BHK luxury apartment offers a rare blend of privacy, light and skyline views. Spread across a generous 1240 sq.ft carpet area, the home features wide-plank flooring, double-glazed windows and an open-plan living-dining space that flows onto a sun-drenched east-facing balcony.

The RERA-approved, Ready-to-Move project is built by a reputed developer and comes with a fully-loaded clubhouse, a rooftop infinity pool and round-the-clock concierge. Located minutes from Bandra-Worli Sea Link, Linking Road and BKC, it is ideal for families seeking a premium address with excellent connectivity, schools and lifestyle options at the doorstep.
